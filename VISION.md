# Vision

Build a durable portfolio of small mobile utilities that people actively search for, use repeatedly, and willingly pay for.

The product is not the code factory. The advantage is better opportunity selection, distribution, quality, and repeated learning.

## Success

Per app: useful daily/weekly job, measurable organic acquisition, strong activation, simple monetization, low maintenance, honest privacy posture and separate brand. Portfolio: positive risk-adjusted net contribution after store fees, tooling and developer time.

## Explicit non-goals

Do not launch look-alike reskins or clone proprietary apps. Do not build AI features without a user outcome. Do not create shared infrastructure in advance of concrete demand. Do not hide low traffic behind impressive technical architecture.

## Portfolio allocation

Run 1–3 experiments concurrently. Establish a stop condition before coding. Preserve good experiments as reusable documentation even when apps are retired.
