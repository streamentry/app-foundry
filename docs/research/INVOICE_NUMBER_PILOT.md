# Folio Invoice Number Ledger — 7-day falsification test

**Prepared:** 2026-11-15. **Status: NOT RUN.** No real user observations, installs, verified search volume, payments or organic conversion measurements.

|---|---|---|

| Offline invoice makers | Often allocate numbers as part of creating and reissuing an actual invoice. | https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aki.offlineinvoicemaker |

**Stronger objection than "competition":** a second app can actually increase duplicate risk when official issuing happens elsewhere. When numbers are allocated on multiple devices or by a team, an on-device-only ledger is the wrong architecture. Folio should not serve that user without an authoritative shared issuer.

## Narrow test audience

Solo operators manually preparing invoices in Word, spreadsheets or multiple incompatible document templates, who have recently had a missing reference, unexplained cancellation or duplicate counter. Ask what they do **before** the demo. Exclude people whose invoicing app already gives a sufficient sequence ledger.

The user and buyer may be the same owner. The client/bookkeeper/regulator can veto if a separate local history is insufficient.

## Exact fictional task for five human participants

Choose prefix INV-2026-, starting counter 215, width four digits. Without coaching:

1. Reserve 215. It should read **INV-2026-0215** and remain RESERVED, not issued.
2. Pretend you issued it in your real external system, then mark it ISSUED.
3. Reserve 216 and VOID it with a recorded cancellation reason.
4. Manually record 220 as already ISSUED, explaining its external source.
5. Confirm 217–219 are **UNACCOUNTED LOCALLY**, not void, and that the next allocation would be 221.

Ask the participant to do the equivalent using their actual current method first or in counterbalanced order. Record both the task time and mistakes. Do not show the answer key while they work.

## Seven-day decision sequence

| Day | Measurable action | Kill signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit 15–20 exact store listings and query suggestions in one country. | No discoverable niche separate from invoice creation. |
| 2 | Founder-led invitations to 10–15 verified solo manual-numbering operators. | Fewer than five qualified users, indicating weak reachability. |
| 3–4 | Five observed simulated reference lifecycles with real comparative tasks. | Two or more cannot complete correctly without coaching. |
| 5 | Ask to demonstrate their last real numbering discrepancy and what would change. | They already have a better authoritative system. |
| 6 | Ask for actual repeat use, manual historic import or concrete paid intent. | Fewer than two costly commitments. |
| 7 | Record GO TO LONGER TRIAL, NARROW or STOP, with observations and counterfactual. | No defensible reason for the extra ledger. |

## Proposed continuation threshold (not established benchmark)

At least 4/5 correct without hints, 3/5 request a second use, 2/5 show a real switching/payment action, measurable benefit over their current method and a plausible country-specific organic query. Zero unresolved duplicate-allocation or backup data-loss bugs.

**Five users cannot establish product-market fit.** Never represent a green test suite as interviews.
