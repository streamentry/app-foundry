# Hours Left — 7-day user falsification pilot

**Operational kit:** [Five-person study runbook](retainer-hours/README.md) with consent, task cards, actual observer instructions, install caveats and an evidence evaluator. No human interviews have yet happened.

**As of 2026-11-15: plan only. No interviews, tests, search-volume results, conversion or payment evidence.**

## Competitive baseline from vendor/App Store claims

- **Retainer Hours Studio (iOS):** one-time $4.99, local retainer board with client packets and scope controls. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/retainer-hours-studio/id6775090274
- **Wrkly (Android):** client/project timer, retained work effective hourly rate and export functionality. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appdaniel.wrkly
- **Billable Call:** confirmed call-time review and prepaid retainer remaining; premium advertised around $3.99/month. https://billablecall.com/
- **Clockify, Harvest, Toggl, My Hours:** general-purpose mature substitutes with reports or integrations. https://www.upwork.com/resources/best-time-tracking-apps-for-freelancers

These are **published feature descriptions**, not firsthand competitor audits. We have no independent App Store keyword volume, installs, revenue estimates, or evidence this exact market is underserved.

## Strongest objection

Freelancers already track time in their billing software. Manual entry into another mobile app can be **extra work**, not value. The hypothesis is that freelancers with 1–5 prepaid-hour clients but weak reconciliation/reporting get meaningful benefit from the separate ledger and a clean client statement. The founder can recruit these users without expensive sales staff.

## Cheap experiment

| Day | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect 10–20 current App Store listings and autocomplete in US/UK plus SEA for retainer hours / prepaid hours / client retainer. | Dated query and competitor gap, not estimated volumes. |
| 2 | Recruit 10–15 actual retainer-based freelancers; ask them to describe the last real reconciliation before showing UI. | Current substitute and frequency; whether they use hours or fixed price. |
| 3–4 | Observe five people reconcile **12h30 prepaid + 2h topup − 11h15 − 4h delivered = 45 minutes over** using both current process and Hours Left. | Unassisted correctness, completion time, errors. |
| 5 | Let users export a hypothetical client statement; ask what is required to trust/share it. | Missing proof, billing approval, export friction. |
| 6 | Ask for a concrete week-long trial, actual import/second period, or paid commitment (no made-up revenue). | At least two meaningful commitments. |
| 7 | Write continue, narrow, or stop. Compare results against their current process, not a hypothetical ideal. | One falsification decision and next action. |

**Proposed pass thresholds, not scientific benchmarks:** 4/5 get the reconciliation correct without coaching; 3/5 request a second period; 2/5 demonstrate meaningful switching or payment effort; and one plausible organic acquisition channel emerges. Any unresolved data loss or incorrect arithmetic is a stop condition.

Five users can reveal usability faults, not validate a market. If this fails, do not build more unrelated features to compensate.
