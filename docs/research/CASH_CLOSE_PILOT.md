# Counter Close — 7-day merchant falsification test

**Status: designed, not executed.** There are no actual interviews, keyword volumes, conversion rates, installations or payment commitments yet.

## Who uses, pays, and vetoes

- **User:** person counting the register (cashier, owner, stall operator).
- **Payer:** usually owner/operator; confirm whether a cashier can choose or recommend tools.
- **Veto:** manager, POS integrator, or bookkeeper who demands certified close records or built-in POS workflow.

## Most likely reason to fail

A calculator, spreadsheet or POS-native cash-close feature may already do this job with *fewer steps*. Design quality cannot rescue a duplicative workflow. Ask to watch the current closing procedure **before** demonstrating Counter Close.

## Seven-day plan

| Day | Concrete action | Potential stop reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record 15–20 competitors for exact store queries in one country; preserve dated screenshots, prices, reviews and autocomplete. | Existing free solutions clearly dominate target workflow. |
| 2 | Founder-led outreach to 10 relevant owner-operated outlets. Ask about the last actual cash close. | Fewer than five had a genuine repeat cash-count job. |
| 3–4 | With permission, watch five people perform simulated closes with coins and notes. Time the task, note errors and record the existing method. | Two or more fail without coaching. |
| 5 | Offer a second count on another day or a different drawer. Compare with their current tool. | Fewer than three voluntarily agree to repeat. |
| 6 | Ask for a meaningful commitment: a week-long trial, switching from their existing workflow or paying for the actual tool if released. | Fewer than two make any real commitment. |
| 7 | Record continue / re-scope / stop and the cheapest next experiment. Do not substitute more code for this evidence. | No observable reason to switch. |

## Proposed pass criteria (not statistical benchmarks)

- At least **4/5** complete a simulated closing without coaching and with correct amounts.
- At least **3/5** want to use it again voluntarily.
- At least **2/5** offer a concrete switching or payment-intent commitment.
- No unresolved arithmetic, data-loss, or privacy defect.
- One defensible country-and-keyword distribution wedge with dated evidence.

Five participants can expose usability problems; they **do not prove product-market fit**. Expand only if early feedback merits it.

Avoid storing a merchant's real till amount, name or identifying photos unless genuinely necessary and explicitly permitted. Simulated banknotes and dummy POS totals are sufficient for an early test.

## Release stop conditions

No store submission or fee collection before native device testing (iOS and Android), backup restore trials, a clear privacy policy, original store artwork, real merchant findings and legal review of the public name. A green JavaScript bundle check is not a signed native app build.
