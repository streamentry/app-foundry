# 2026-11-15 — Why Retainer Hours Ledger is our second promoted candidate

**Decision:** build one differentiated, offline-first prepaid-hours reconciliation app for independent consultants/freelancers. **Not** permission to publish or collect payment without real feedback.

The subjective original list scored Retainer Hours #097 at **80**, below Invoice Number Ledger, Contract Renewals and Math Mistake Notebook (all **87**). Scores were never measured search volume, competition or profit forecasts. The higher numeric value should not be treated as a conclusion.

**Relative counterfactuals (product source descriptions, November 2026):**
- Invoice Number Ledger: likely unnecessary when client already uses invoicing software that assigns sequences; needs proof of genuine exception.
- Contract Renewals: valuable consequences, but dedicated notice-period reminder/AI document competitors already exist and an incorrect missed reminder creates legal/financial tail risk.
- Moving Box Inventory: compelling episodic use, but QR/voice/photo/printing products already serve the common workflow.
- Math Error Notebook: recurring education use and at least one competitor with 10K+ Play installs, but photo scanning, recall and review schedulers already exist; student willingness to pay is unproven.
- Retainer Hours: has direct work-value loss, founder-accessible user group, pure offline integer-minute ledger. But established time trackers and new retainer-specific products provide substitutes, so switching friction is material.

**Three carrying hypotheses:** some freelancers lack a clear prepaid-hours balance; a separate mobile ledger is faster than existing workflow; at least two will commit effort or payment after a five-person study.

**Kill condition:** can't demonstrate time/error improvement, can't recruit qualified real users, no hard commitment, or no credible organic search route. Building code is only a low-cost experiment, not validation. See [pilot](../../research/RETAINER_HOURS_PILOT.md).
